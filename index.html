How to print

  1. Print the pitch test on the same printer and paper. Lay the lens on it with the ridges along the lines. The bar that looks one solid colour and flips evenly is your real LPI. Type it in above.
  2. Print the magnet or sheet at 100% / actual size. Turn off "fit to page". Glossy photo paper, best quality.
  3. Let the ink dry about an hour. Line the lens ridges up with the strips, turn it slightly until one picture looks clean, then press it down from one edge.
  4. Stick it on magnet sheet and cut on the crop marks.
